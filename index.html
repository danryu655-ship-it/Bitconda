Why Is This Challenging? — Rank Explained

Minting Bitconda is not just a click. Every mint must solve a puzzle first: find a nonce so that hash(wallet, tokenId, nonce) has the required leading zero bits. The more people mint, the harder the puzzle gets.

RankWhy It's Challenging
🥉 Uncommon (60%)The starting point. The lightest puzzle, but you still have to solve it — no free click.
🥈 Rare (25%)Rarer, with more detail. Patient solvers in the early phases have a better shot.
🥇 Epic (10%)Only 500 out of 5,000. A harder puzzle + blinking lightning animation. Getting an Epic proves you beat a tougher challenge.
👑 Legend (5%)Only 250 out of 5,000 — the top rank. The hardest puzzle + full animation (eyes, tongue, and active aura). This is the Bitconda trophy.

Simple rule: the fastest + strongest puzzle solvers get the top ranks. Once all 5,000 are gone, there is no more minting.

Price — Paid in BNB, Not USDT

The $1 / $3 / $5 prices are converted directly to BNB at deployment, and payment is in native BNB (not USDT, not another token). Minters just need BNB for the price + gas — no need to buy USDT first.

Conversion example (not the final numbers, recalculated at deployment from the BNB price at that time): if BNB is ~$703, then $1 ≈ 0.00142 BNB, $3 ≈ 0.00427 BNB, $5 ≈ 0.00711 BNB. The final numbers in the contract are fixed BNB amounts set by the owner at deployment.
